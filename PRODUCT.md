# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Developers and content creators managing drafts, reading posts, and tracking user signups in a full-stack CI/CD sandbox.

## Product Purpose
To serve as a high-performance feed and management tool for developer-driven content (posts, drafts, and users). Success is a highly responsive, clean, and intuitive developer workflow interface.

## Brand Personality
Technical, sleek, high-fidelity. Evokes focus, developer productivity, and developer confidence.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered/overstimulating dashboards with flashing metrics or gradients.
- SaaS-cream/beige-minimalist templates (avoiding the saturated AI cream bg).
- Generic flat, gray, low-contrast designs.

## Design Principles
- **Expert Confidence**: Present information and controls in a clean, unambiguous layout designed for technical professionals.
- **Focus on Content**: Minimize peripheral distractions to let the posts and code speak for themselves.
- **Fluid Response**: Provide clear, instantaneous feedback for every interaction without layout shifts or unnecessary loader lag.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. High text contrast (≥ 4.5:1), visible keyboard focus states, and respect for user preferences like `prefers-reduced-motion`.
